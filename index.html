Why FoodieCafe

One counter, six kitchens

We started as a single dosa cart and grew into a food court where six kitchens share one billing counter. You order once, and everything lands on the same tray at the same time. No arguing about cuisine.

🥫

Bought fresh daily

Produce from the Mandai market every morning at 6, nothing held overnight.

⏰

Under 15 minutes

Tray on your table inside 15 minutes, or the meal is on the house.

🌱

Separate veg line

Dedicated veg kitchen with its own oil, pans and prep boards. Never shared.

🧺

Open kitchen

Every station is behind glass. Watch your food get cooked while you wait.
